Analysis & Critical Evaluation by Sebastien

When creating an effective health plan, several critical considerations must be taken into account to ensure the goals are structured properly and actually achievable. First, setting goals requires utilizing the S.M.A.R.T. framework, which ensures objectives are specific, measurable, attainable, realistic, and timely (page 4). As the reading emphasizes, "a specific goal has a much greater chance of being accomplished than a general goal" (page 4). In addition, setting measurable criteria gives concrete feedback so that individuals can track progress and stay motivated (pages 4–5). Finally, goals must be realistic and grounded within a clear time frame, because "with no time frame tied to it there's no sense of urgency" (page 5).

Considerations for Creating a Health Plan

Creating a successful health plan also requires evaluating whether a goal is attainable and realistic based on personal capabilities and resources (page 5). Research shows that specific and challenging goals lead to better performance overall (page 6). Furthermore, establishing a tangible goal allows one to experience progress through the senses, which makes the objective much easier to measure and attain (page 6).

Personal Health Plan: Improving Sleep and Physical Wellness

To put these principles into practice, I have developed a personal health plan designed to address my current strengths, needs, and risks regarding physical wellness and daily energy management.

Strengths, Needs, and Risks: My primary strength is consistency in maintaining structured daily routines. My current need is to improve evening sleep hygiene to eliminate minor late-day fatigue. The risk I need to address is the potential burnout caused by balancing late-night screen time with early morning responsibilities.

S.M.A.R.T. Goal Statement: "I will disconnect from all non-academic electronic screens and caffeine by 9:00 PM every night for the next 6 weeks, allowing me to achieve 8 hours of consistent nightly sleep by November 24, 2026."

Plan Action Steps and Monitoring

To ensure this health plan succeeds, I will track my nightly sleep duration and morning energy levels using a daily log. By breaking down the larger objective into weekly milestones and maintaining an established routine, I can ensure steady progress toward optimal physical and mental wellness (page 5).
