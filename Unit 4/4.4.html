Adolescent years involve immense physical, emotional, and social changes where testing independence and experimenting with new behaviors can introduce significant health risks (page 2, iBook part two). Engaging in certain health risk behaviors can greatly increase the likelihood of sliding into further unhealthy choices, such as poor nutrition, sleep deprivation, low mood, and substance use (page 2, iBook part two). To counteract these risks, maintaining balanced habits is vital; for instance, exercise is associated with a more positive mood, improved sleep, better concentration, and higher self-esteem (page 2, iBook part two). Furthermore, public health policies and government regulations establish vital standards for health promotion and disease prevention by guiding community environments and safety norms.

Analyzing Health Risks and Public Health Policies

When individuals neglect basic wellness foundations—such as prioritizing fast food and sugary sports drinks over proper nutrition—it creates a cascading effect that fails to provide lasting energy for proper growth and development (pages 2–3, iBook part two). In addition, excessive screen time and sedentary habits promote long-term risks like obesity (page 3, iBook part two). Government regulations and public health policies help mitigate these widespread risks by shaping educational programs, environmental safety, and community wellness frameworks.

Personal Situation Analysis: Supporting Positive Health Choices

Demonstrating how to influence and support others to make positive health choices can be achieved through active peer engagement. A personal situation where this occurred involved helping a friend who was struggling with a sedentary routine and sleep deprivation due to excessive late-night screen time and poor dietary habits during a busy school project.

Rather than lecturing, I supported my friend by opening up an honest dialogue about health, inviting them to join me in daily physical activities like biking and running with our pets, and helping them substitute sugary snacks with balanced options (pages 2–3, iBook part two).

The Outcome: By keeping the lines of communication open and modeling positive habits, my friend successfully reduced their daily screen time, improved their sleep schedule, and experienced a noticeable boost in energy, concentration, and overall mood (pages 2–3, iBook part two).
